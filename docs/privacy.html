Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 7, 2026

Information We Collect

Volarm is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal data:

  • Alarm Data: Your alarm settings (time, volume, sound, repeat schedule) are stored locally on your device using SwiftData. This data never leaves your device.
  • Feedback Data: If you choose to contact support through the app, we collect the information you voluntarily provide: your name (optional), email address, and message content.

How We Use Your Information

  • Alarm data is used solely to deliver your scheduled alarms at the correct times and volumes.
  • Feedback data is used to respond to your support requests and improve the app.

Data Storage

All alarm data is stored locally on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. We do not have access to your alarm data. No alarm data is transmitted to our servers.

Third-Party Services

Volarm uses the following Apple services:

  • AlarmKit: For scheduling and delivering system-level alarms.
  • StoreKit: For processing in-app purchases. Payment processing is handled entirely by Apple.

We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal data with any third parties.

Data Retention

  • Alarm data is retained on your device until you delete the alarm or uninstall the app.
  • Feedback data is retained only as long as necessary to resolve your support request.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Delete any alarm at any time by swiping left on it in the app.
  • Delete all app data by uninstalling the app from your device.
  • Request deletion of any feedback data by contacting us.

Children's Privacy

Volarm does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
