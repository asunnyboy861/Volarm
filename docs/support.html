Volarm Support

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I set a different volume for each alarm?

When creating or editing an alarm, use the volume slider to set the volume level. Each alarm stores its own volume independently. The color of the slider and indicator shows the volume level: Blue (Quiet), Green (Medium), Orange (Loud), Red (Maximum).

Will my alarms work in Silent Mode?

Yes! Volarm uses Apple's AlarmKit framework, which delivers alarms at the system level. Your alarms will ring even when your phone is in Silent Mode or Focus Mode.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Go to Settings (gear icon) and tap "Restore Purchases". Your Pro features will be restored immediately.

What's included in the free version?

The free version includes up to 3 alarms with independent volume control, 5 built-in sounds, and basic snooze functionality. Upgrade to Pro for unlimited alarms, gradual volume wake-up, and more premium features.

What is Gradual Volume?

Gradual Volume starts the alarm at a low volume and slowly increases it over 10-60 seconds. This provides a gentler wake-up experience compared to a sudden loud alarm. This feature requires Volarm Pro.

Why does Volarm need alarm permission?

Volarm needs alarm permission to schedule system-level alarms that ring reliably even in Silent Mode. Without this permission, alarms cannot be delivered. You can grant this permission during onboarding or in your device Settings.

How do I delete an alarm?

Swipe left on any alarm in the list and tap the red Delete button.
