## Description
Finally, an alarm clock where every alarm has its own volume.

On iPhone, all alarms share the same Ringtone and Alerts volume. Want a blaring alarm for workdays but a gentle one for weekends? You are out of luck with the built-in Clock app. Volarm fixes this.

Each alarm in Volarm has its own independent volume slider. Set your 6 AM weekday alarm to maximum so you never oversleep, and your 9 AM weekend alarm to a soft 20 percent so you wake up gently. No more choosing between sleeping through your alarm or waking the whole house.

COLOR-CODED VOLUME
See volume at a glance. Each alarm shows a color that matches its volume level:
- Blue for Quiet
- Green for Medium
- Orange for Loud
- Red for Maximum
No more guessing which alarm is which. One look at the list tells you everything.

GRADUAL VOLUME WAKE-UP
Start quiet and slowly increase. Volarm can begin at a low volume and gently ramp up over 10 to 60 seconds. A peaceful way to wake up without the jarring shock of a sudden loud alarm. Perfect for light sleepers and anyone who hates being startled awake.

SYSTEM-LEVEL RELIABILITY
Powered by Apple AlarmKit. Your alarms break through Silent Mode and Focus Mode. Even if your phone is on silent, Volarm delivers your alarm at the volume you set. No more missed wake-ups because you forgot to turn off silent.

BUILT-IN SOUNDS
Choose from a curated collection of alarm sounds designed to wake you up effectively. From gentle chimes to attention-grabbing tones, find the perfect sound for each alarm.

REPEAT AND SCHEDULE
Set alarms for specific days of the week or create one-time alarms. Your schedule, your rules. Each alarm remembers its own volume, sound, and schedule independently.

SIRI AND SHORTCUTS
Control your alarms with your voice. Use Siri to set, enable, or disable alarms hands-free. Integrate with Shortcuts for automated routines.

- No subscriptions ever
